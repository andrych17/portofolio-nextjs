VIDEO 02 — Preloader: Monogram "AH" terbentuk dari sinyal
Meniru loading ww.cons (construction-landing-web/ArchitecturalPreloader.tsx):
video bumper logo full-screen ±2.5 detik → tirai naik → situs tampil.

Cerita: sama dengan video hero 01 (Signal Paths). Pulsa sinyal oranye datang dari
tepi frame lewat jalur-jalur lurus seperti trace sirkuit, bertemu di tengah, lalu
menggambar monogram — seperti sistem yang "boot" dan menyusun identitas Anda.
Preloader → hero jadi satu bahasa visual.

MODE: Frames to Video (first frame + last frame). BUKAN text-to-video.

Unggah (ada di folder ini):
  First frame : logo-first-frame-16x9.png   (hitam polos #060609)
  Last frame  : logo-end-frame-16x9.png     (monogram "AH" asli)
Durasi: 8 detik.

Kenapa first + last frame: bagian tengah boleh liar (sinyal, garis cahaya),
tapi frame TERAKHIR dikunci ke gambar logo asli — jadi hasil akhirnya pasti
identik dengan monogram di favicon. Prompt tidak menyebut huruf/bentuk; menyebut
bentuk justru memancing model menggambar ulang.


=== PROMPT ========================================================

Total darkness. Thin lines of hot orange light (#ff4d00) enter from the edges of
the frame and travel inward along straight paths with clean 90-degree and
45-degree turns, like signals racing along the traces of a circuit board,
leaving faint glowing trails behind them. The signals converge at the center
of the frame, where they draw the strokes of the final mark with precise,
confident motion, like a laser plotter. The trails behind fade out, the mark
glows briefly brighter, then a single clean white highlight sweeps once across
it from left to right, and everything settles into complete stillness and
holds. Deep black background throughout, no background change. Precise,
engineered, premium tech-noir brand ident. Subtle 35mm film grain. One
continuous take, no cuts.

=== CATATAN =======================================================

1. Yang wajib sempurna: 2–3 detik TERAKHIR (logo sudah jadi & diam), latar kembali
   hitam polos tanpa sisa jalur. Cek frame terakhir identik dengan logo-end-frame.
   Bagian tengah (sinyal bergerak) boleh abstrak.

2. Potong untuk preloader (±2.8 detik): ambil dari saat garis cahaya mulai
   bergerak sampai logo diam, biasanya detik ~3.5 s/d ~6.3:

   ffmpeg -ss 3.5 -t 2.8 -i hasil_flow.mp4 -an -vf scale=1280:-2 -c:v libx264 \
     -crf 24 -preset slow -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart public/videos/logo.mp4

   Durasi potongan ikut timing video — kirim video mentahnya, saya pilih titik potong.
   Target ≤ 800 KB.

3. Kalau logo di frame terakhir tidak identik, generate ulang.



=== PEMASANGAN (saya kerjakan setelah video dikirim) ==============

- video full-screen `autoPlay muted playsInline preload="auto"`, latar #060609
- selesai → tirai naik (y: -100%, ease [0.76, 0, 0.24, 1]), sama seperti ww.cons
- `onError` → fallback ke preloader sekarang (monogram + counter %)
- `prefers-reduced-motion` → preloader dilewati; tetap sekali per sesi
